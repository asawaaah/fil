Big news about rivers

Share on X Share on Facebook

Rivers across the region have reached their highest level in decades, according to a report published on Monday, and scientists say the trend will continue.

The report, which gathered measurements from more than 300 stations, shows that the average flow increased by 12 percent, while some smaller streams doubled their volume.

What experts say

Experts point to changing rainfall patterns, faster snow melt and land use as the main causes, although they disagree on the exact share of each.

  • More rain in spring
  • Earlier snow melt
We have never seen numbers like these, said one hydrologist, who asked not to be named.

Local authorities have started to reinforce dikes in the most exposed towns, and a new warning system will be tested this summer.
